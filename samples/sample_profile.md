### [Project A] D2C E-Commerce Conversion Funnel Optimization & ROAS Enhancement
* **Context**: Low mobile landing page conversion rate (CVR 1.2%) despite strong ad click-through rates (CTR 4.1%) during a major seasonal campaign.
* **Problem & Dilemma**: High ad spend was leaking at the product option selection and checkout transition modal. Simply redesigning the page risked alienating loyal desktop users and disrupting existing tracking event tags.
* **Process & Action**:
  * Cross-analyzed Hotjar session recordings and GA4 user event funnels to identify mobile UX bottlenecks (unresponsive modal overlays and confusing coupon application order).
  * Designed an A/B/n test matrix (Control vs. Sticky Option Bar vs. One-Click Quick Checkout) and ran experiments across 50,000 unique visitors over 2 weeks with a 95% statistical confidence threshold.
  * Synchronized drop-off event triggers with automated Kakao AlimTalk retargeting sequences for users abandoning carts within 30 minutes.
* **Result**: Improved mobile landing page CVR from 1.2% to 3.4% (+183%), achieving campaign ROAS of 320%.
